Maru Mail Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-30 · Applies to the Maru Mail desktop application published at getmaru.app

Maru Mail ("Maru") is a desktop email client that runs on your computer. This policy describes exactly what data Maru touches, where it goes, and how you delete it. The short version: your mail stays on your device, Maru operates no servers, and nothing is collected about you.

What Google data Maru accesses

When you connect a Gmail account, Maru requests the gmail.modify permission from Google. Maru uses it to:

  • Read your threads, messages, headers, bodies, attachments, labels, and mailbox change history, to display and search your mail.
  • Change labels — archive, star, mark read or unread, apply your own labels, and move messages to and from Trash. Maru never permanently deletes messages or bypasses Trash.
  • Send messages that you compose, or that you explicitly approve after an agent requests them.

Where your data is stored

  • Messages, threads, and attachment metadata are stored in an encrypted local database on your device, one encryption key per account, held in your operating system's keychain. Attachment contents are fetched from Gmail when you open or save them, not kept in the database.
  • OAuth tokens (the credentials Maru uses to talk to Gmail) are stored in your operating system's keychain.
  • Search indexes, settings, agent grants, send approvals, and the audit log are stored locally on your device. Audit entries that contain Google-derived content (such as message subjects) are encrypted with per-account keys.

Maru operates no server. Your mail data is never transmitted to the developer of Maru or to any Maru-operated service. The only network peer for your mail is Google.

Agents, and data that can leave your device

Maru can optionally act as a gateway for AI agents you choose to connect. If — and only if — you create an agent credential, grant that agent capabilities, and consent to an agent session, Maru will return the mail data that agent requests to it over a local connection on your device.

The agent you connect is a separate program, and it may send data to its own model provider for processing outside your device. Maru discloses this before you consent, and shows you which agent is asking and what it may access. Review your agent's and its provider's terms before granting access. Sending a message always requires your separate, explicit approval inside Maru, regardless of any grant.

What Maru does not do

  • Maru collects no telemetry, analytics, or crash reports. The developer learns nothing when you use Maru.
  • Maru does not use your Google data to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Maru does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. There is no one on the other end to share it with.

Limited Use disclosure

Maru's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Maru uses Google user data only to provide the user-facing mail features described in this policy, transfers it only as directed by you (to an agent you connected and consented to), and never uses it for advertising or for training generalized AI/ML models.

Retention and deletion

  • Mail is retained locally for as long as the account is connected, so the app works offline.
  • Removing an account in Settings deletes its cached mail, search index entries, and tokens from your device, and destroys the encryption keys for its audit content.
  • You can additionally revoke Maru's access at Google any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • A step-by-step guide is at getmaru.app/support/google-data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and the history is public in Maru's source repository. Questions: support@getmaru.app.
