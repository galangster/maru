Maru stores mail on your device, so most mail-cache problems affect one machine. This page reports shared incidents involving Google's OAuth or API services and the optional Maru account service.

What degraded means

Degraded means the account service cannot reliably read or accept encrypted vault updates, device changes, push notices, or billing state. Local mail and direct Gmail access can continue during a Maru account incident.

If something happens

An active incident appears here with its start time, affected functions, next update time, and any action you must take. An operator updates this static page by hand. Service health is also checked through the Railway deployment at /healthz.
