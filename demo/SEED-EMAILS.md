# Demo account seed mail

Send these to the demo account (galangsterr@gmail.com) before recording
so the mailbox looks real without exposing anyone's actual mail. Send
them from any account; ordinary consumer content, nothing sensitive.

1. **Subject: Team lunch Thursday?** — "Thinking Thai place on 5th at
   12:30. Who's in?" (reply once from the demo account so one thread
   has two messages — gives shot 04 a real conversation)
2. **Subject: Q3 reading list** — a few book titles in the body.
   **Attach any small PDF** (shot 04 opens an attachment).
3. **Subject: Your weekend photos** — one line, attach a JPG.
4. **Subject: Invoice #1042 from Acme Design** — "Attached is the
   invoice for August. Net 30." (star this one — shot 04 shows a
   starred thread; shot 05 labels/archives it)
5. **Subject: Newsletter: The Maker's Digest #12** — two short
   paragraphs of filler (shot 05 trashes and untrashes this one)

Shot 06 (the human send) and shot 09 (the agent send) both send FROM
the demo account — address them to your main account so nothing leaves
the household.
