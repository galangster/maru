## What shipped

Save a thread for later and it leaves the inbox until its time. Local to this
Mac, in its own `thread_defer` table (migration 6, `SCHEMA_VERSION` 6), with a
lazy predicate instead of a timer.

The reasoning is in three places and does not need repeating here: the commit
message `4d77b1c`, the build log at the bottom of
`wayfinder/tickets/P21-later-and-swipe.md`, and the amended **Q17** in
`docs/DECISIONS.md`. The short version is that the defence is the *failure
mode*, not privacy: a label-based snooze removes INBOX at Google and needs a
network write at wake time that only happens if this Mac runs, so a laptop shut
Monday to Friday hides mail on every device past its time with no timer
anywhere to fix it. Label-based fails unsafe; local-only fails safe.

**It calls no new Gmail method.** `docs/security/google-oauth-method-scope-matrix.md`
and the verification answers are untouched, and that was a reason to prefer
this shape rather than a side effect of it.

## Five differences from the ticket's spec

All five are recorded in the ticket's build log with reasons. The two worth
knowing before touching this code:

1. **`MailService` gained THREE methods, not one.** The spec has `defer` alone
   and has `useWakeSweep` calling `store.sweepDeferrals(now)` — but the UI
   layer only ever holds a `MailService` and cannot reach the store. So the
   sweep is `wakeDeferred(now)` on the seam, and the sidebar's count is
   `deferredCount()`. The count is its own method rather than the length of the
   Later list because that list is capped at a page, and a badge that stops
   counting at 100 is a badge that lies quietly.

2. **The Later view groups by WAKE time.** Grouping by the sort key in a list
   ordered by `wake_at` produced headers that disagreed with their own order —
   two threads returning tomorrow and Monday both filed under "Yesterday", on
   the first screenshot. `wakeGroup` in `lib/format.ts` is a second closed set,
   closed at a month because `MAX_DEFER_DAYS` is 30.

## Two defects the work surfaced, both fixed

- **`dateGroup` is a past-tense bucketer with no upper bound**, so its first
  branch swallows every future timestamp. Reusing it made `wakeTime` report
  "tomorrow, 9:00" as "this evening, 9:00", and it is the same root cause as
  difference 2 above. `wakeTime` now measures whole days between local
  midnights, which also makes it right across a DST day. **If you add anything
  else that formats a future time, do not reach for `dateGroup`.**

- **The bulk bar overflowed at a sixth verb** — measured 444 px in the list
  pane's default 400, which pushed `Clear` clean off the end. Re-spaced to
  `gap-1.5 px-3`; the worst case is "99 selected · All 100" at 407 px, 7 px
  into the 12 px right padding, nothing clipped. **There is no room left.** A
  seventh verb needs a wrap or one fewer verb, and the comment in
  `thread-list.tsx` says so.
