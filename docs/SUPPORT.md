# Support macros

Use these replies from `support@getmaru.app`. Replace bracketed text before you send a reply.

## Lost recovery key

**Subject:** Your Maru recovery key

Maru cannot retrieve or replace a lost recovery key. The service does not have a key that can open your vault.

If you still know your password, keep it safe. If you lose both your password and your recovery key, your vault is unrecoverable. Support cannot help recover it.

## Revoke a device

**Subject:** Remove a device from your Maru account

Open Maru account settings on a signed-in device. Find the old device and select **Sign out**.

This action revokes that device's Maru session when it next contacts the service. It does not delete Google tokens already stored on that device. Use **Delete local data** on the device if you still control it.

If you do not recognize the device, revoke it now. Then change your Maru password.

## Delete account

**Subject:** Delete your Maru account

Open Maru account settings. Export any data that you want to keep. Then select **Delete account** and confirm the action.

You can export and delete through the app. We purge deleted account data from the service within 30 days.

Removing a Gmail account is separate. Use the local Google-data controls if you also want to remove cached mail and Google tokens from a device.

## Refund

**Subject:** Maru refund request

We provide refunds on request within fourteen days of the first charge.

Please send the request from the email on your Maru account. Include the charge date and whether you used the monthly or yearly plan. Do not send card details.

We will reply when Stripe records the refund.

## The unverified-app screen

**Subject:** Google says Maru is unverified

Google can show an unverified-app warning before it completes Maru's verification. The warning applies to Maru's Google OAuth client. It does not mean that Maru received your Google password.

Continue only if you downloaded Maru from getmaru.app or the official GitHub repository. You must also have a private-beta invitation. Otherwise, stop and email support.

If both conditions apply, select **Advanced**. Then select the link that continues to Maru Mail. Google limits the unverified client to approved beta users.

## My Later did not show on my phone

**Subject:** Later does not sync yet

Later is not yet synced between devices. A thread that you defer on your Mac can remain in the phone's inbox.

Ticket A9 tracks encrypted Later sync. Until that ships, manage Later separately on each device.
