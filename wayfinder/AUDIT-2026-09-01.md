## 4. What Nick had not raised

Each is decided or queued; none is left implicit.

1. **Later does not cross devices.** P21 stores deferrals in a local table on
   purpose (fails safe). With two devices that is a bug: a thread saved for
   Monday on the Mac is still in the phone's inbox. Recommendation: sync
   deferrals inside the encrypted vault as `{threadId, until}` pairs. That
   sends thread *ids* as ciphertext, which the server cannot read, but it
   moves the letter of "no ids reach the service" (spec §1). **Owner
   decision, queued (A9).** My judgement: do it; a Later that works on one
   device is worse than none.
2. **Legal pages.** The App Store, Stripe and the Google review all require
   a privacy policy URL; Stripe and the trial need terms. Drafts are ticket
   A6; a lawyer's read is queued.
3. **Breach notification.** A custodian owes a written plan: what is
   compromised under each scenario (server, build pipeline, operator
   account), who is told, how, within what time. A8.
4. **Backups are not backups until restored.** A restore drill is part of
   A5's acceptance.
5. **Two-factor on every account that can ship a build or move money:**
   GitHub, Apple, Google Cloud, Railway, Stripe, npm (already forced). Queued.
6. **Export compliance.** The phone uses AES-GCM through the system crypto;
   declare `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` under the standard-crypto
   exemption. I6.
7. **App Review needs a working account.** A reviewer must be able to sign in
   to Gmail and to Maru. A dedicated Google account and a comped Maru account
   are queued for Nick, because both need a human's phone number.
8. **A bad sync overwrites settings.** Spec §13 keeps ten vault versions and
   a restore endpoint, so this is recoverable without the server reading.
9. **Refunds and tax.** Fourteen-day trial, then refunds on request within
   fourteen days of the first charge; Stripe Tax collects, but registrations
   are Nick's. A3.
10. **Gmail watch expiry.** `users.watch` lasts seven days and the phone
    cannot renew in the background reliably. The renewal runs on app open,
    on background refresh, and on every silent push. I4.
11. **The keychain on the phone** must use "after first unlock" accessibility
    or background fetch fails after a reboot. I4.
12. **Losing the recovery key is losing the vault.** The ceremony copy says
    it in one sentence; there is no support path that can help, and support
    macros must say so. A6.
13. **Cost floor.** Railway plus Postgres runs about $10–20 a month at beta
    scale; Stripe's 30¢ makes monthly plans thin, which is why the product
    steers to annual (Q25).
14. **The unverified-app screen.** Until Google grants verification, strangers
    see a warning and restricted scopes cap at 100 users. Q1 accepted this.
