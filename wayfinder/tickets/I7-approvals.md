status: **after map 5 v1** · needs its own grill

The approvals queue lives on the desktop gateway. Carrying it to the phone
means the desktop publishes pending approvals to the account (ciphertext),
the phone answers, and the desktop acts. Grants still never sync; only the
decision travels. Design before build.
